# Responsible-use policy

This product exists to improve engineering systems, not to score people.

- Metrics are aggregated to repositories, teams, or the organization.
- Small cohorts are suppressed before display or export.
- Individual rankings and composite productivity scores are prohibited.
- Commit count, lines changed, working hours, and after-hours activity are not performance measures.
- Every metric exposes its definition, source evidence, freshness, exclusions, and limitations.
- Reliability proxies are hidden when the necessary deployment linkage is incomplete.
- Dashboard data must not be the sole basis for an employment decision.
- The MVP does not retain source code, diff contents, PR bodies, issue bodies, or comments.
